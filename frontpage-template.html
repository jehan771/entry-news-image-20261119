TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN HOLD FIRST-EVER THREE-WAY SUMMIT

Xi Jinping chaired a first-ever trilateral summit with Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen on Thursday, on the sidelines of APEC. After about 90 minutes they issued a joint statement — UN reform, more refined-petroleum supply, a warning to the ICC — and reaffirmed that “a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought”. No breakthrough on Ukraine or Iran.
